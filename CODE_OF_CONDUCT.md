# Code of conduct

This is a small modding project, and the rules are simple:

- Be respectful to everyone, whatever their experience with modding.
- Keep discussions about the project. No harassment, insults or personal
  attacks.
- Don't post other people's personal data, or files and mods you don't have
  the right to share.

Comments or issues that break these rules may be edited, closed or removed,
and repeat offenders blocked. If something bothers you, contact me through my
[Nexus Mods profile](https://www.nexusmods.com/profile/ArialSenki).

Based on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
