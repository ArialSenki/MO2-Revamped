Sources in modorganizer_super are snapshots, without history, of the
ModOrganizer2 repositories at the commits of the Mod Organizer 2.5.2 release
(Mod.Organizer-2.5.2-commits.txt, a release asset of ModOrganizer2/modorganizer v2.5.2).
The repository of <name> is https://github.com/ModOrganizer2/modorganizer-<name>,
except cmake_common, which is https://github.com/ModOrganizer2/cmake_common.
Changes made for MO2 Revamped are in later commits of this repository.
lootcli's src/ is from a later commit (MO2's port to libloot 0.29), not 2.5.2's.
